# How Clotr is funded and run

Clotr is free. I'm (BilliamBaSH) currently the only one that maintains and runs it, and I pay for it out of pocket. This page says where any money comes from, where it goes and who decides, so nobody has to guess.

## Where money can come from

| Source | What it is | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Donations and sponsorships ([Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/billiambash)) | Gifts to the project | Costs first, then hours, as set out below |
| Paid services | My own time: setup help, check-ups, training, support | Mine personally until an LLC or business exists |
| Paid separate tools | Software I write alone that sits on top of Clotr | Same as paid services |
| A fiscal host (later) | An outside group that holds a project's money and reports it in the open | I'll apply when the community is big enough to be accepted. Donations would move there. |

**Nothing you contribute is part of anything I sell.** See [COMMITMENTS.md](COMMITMENTS.md).

## What donations pay for, in order

1. Real costs first: store fees, the domain, tools and legal review.
2. My hours on Clotr, at a published rate of $20/hour or for milestones I write down before I start.
3. Paying outside contributors for agreed milestones, once there's money for it. They keep their copyright either way.
4. Whatever's left stays in reserve for next year's costs.

## Keeping myself honest

I'm one person and I could potentially pay myself from donations, so I'm going to go by the following rules:

- The rate or the milestone gets written down before the work starts.
- Once a year I publish a short note: money in by source, money out by category, and what I was paid for.
- I won't pay myself from donations for anything I haven't logged, publically.
- When a fiscal host takes over, its public reports replace my note.

## Business and taxes

Right now money comes to me personally. Before I take payment for services I'll get advice from a VA/VRE clinic or an attorney, and set money aside for taxes. Money has never, and will never, be the goal. As such, a lot of this will be refined if it ever needs to be.

## Who decides

- I'm the creator/maintainer and I make the final call on what goes into Clotr.
- Anyone can propose a change in an issue. If I say no, I'll tell you why.
- Decisions that affect the commitments get written down in the repository.
- [COMMITMENTS.md](COMMITMENTS.md) binds me too. It can be widened but never narrowed.

## If I stop

See section 7 of [COMMITMENTS.md](COMMITMENTS.md).

I'm a solo developer, not an accountant or a lawyer. If something here is unclear, open an issue and tell me. I'm a guy, and I'm open to just talking.
