          How Clotr responds

          Tourniquet keeps some kinds stricter. While it's on, the choices below what it keeps can't be picked.

          Warn: a note in the corner, sending isn't stopped · Ask before sending: you choose before it goes out · Just count: no interruption, still shown in your history.

          Your vault

          Your name, family, addresses, phones, emails, employer and ID formats. Clotr watches for them however they're written and only saves a scrambled code, never the details themselves.

          Client names, project codenames, or a format like EMP-#####. Clotr warns you when one shows up.

          If your vault has details in it, Clotr also reads the AI's answer for 90 seconds after you send, and tells you if it brings one up that you didn't type. It only keeps what kind of detail it was, never the text.

          Bandage

          On a site where you say yes, personal details become cover names like [Phone 1] while you type, so the AI never sees the real thing. Point at a label in its answer (or focus it with the keyboard) to see the real detail.
