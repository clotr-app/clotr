# Health, warn: made-up English messages where a health term is said about a person AND the message says who that
# person is (the detection design, 3.4 and 4.1). When the health kind ships (1.5.0, and only if these corpora
# pass), every one must warn as a health detail, and Hide it must take out who the person is, not the health words.
# Every name, number and address is made up: phones are 555-01xx, emails use example.com, .org or .net.
# Each message starts with one label line, then the message:
#   [term: <the health term exactly as written> | who: <comma list>]
# "who" is a name introduced in the text ("my name is…", "I'm Maria Lopez", "Patient: …", "my daughter Ana Ruiz") or
# a kind today's detector finds: date_of_birth, street_address, phone_number, email, us_ssn, medical_record,
# medicare_id, insurance_id, passport, drivers_license, national_id, student_id. Each term sits within six words after
# its anchor ("I have", "diagnosed with", "my son has", "I take", "Diagnosis:"). Two terms are misspelled on purpose
# (diabeties, anxeity). Messages are separated by lines of "---". tests/health-study.test.js checks the labels, and
# that today only the identity parts warn.
