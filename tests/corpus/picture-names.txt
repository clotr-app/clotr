# Names of pictures and scanned files people attach to AI chats, for the name check (pictures.js, nameClues).
# One name per line: "+ " says the picture is a photo of an ID or a document, so Clotr should warn; "- " doesn't,
# so a warning would be a false alarm. Made-up names in the shapes cameras, phones, scanners and people use, in
# English and Spanish: camera and screenshot names, everyday pictures, and names about IDs that aren't one (a
# template, a logo, a license plate, a software license). Held to the floor (no worse than 1 in 50 either way)
# by tests/pictures.test.js, and to the ratchet by the scorecard. Leave out names that could honestly go either way.
