# Everyday messages that look like scams, in English: real sign-in texts that say they'll never ask for the code, real
# bank alerts with no ask, delivery updates, a meeting moved, a surprise party, a grandchild asking for a ride, a tax
# reminder, a gift card for a birthday, a friend asking for the wifi password, the IT team, a raffle at work. Every one
# is made up: phone numbers are 555-01xx, links and emails use example.com or example.invalid, and every person, bank
# and shop is invented (only the gift card and app brands people really name are real).
# Each message starts with one label line, then the message. Most are [signs: none]. A few honestly have a sign in them
# (a surprise party says to keep it secret, a coworker asks for something right away, a friend asks to be paid back by
# a payment app), and the label lists it:
#   [signs: press_secret]
# The target (tests/scam-signs.test.js, and the scorecard): no sign the label doesn't list, ever. Every listed sign
# must be found too, so a label can't hide a sign that went missing.
# Messages are separated by lines of "---".
