# Code of conduct

Clotr follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It covers the issues, pull requests, reviews and anywhere else someone speaks for the project.

In short, be kind and patient with people, especially the ones reporting a problem for the first time. Plenty of the
people Clotr is for aren't technical, and a report written in everyday words is just as welcome as one with a stack
trace. Harassment, insults, and posting someone's private details are never okay here, and that includes the
details of whoever sent a scam message you're quoting.

## Reporting a problem

Email me at [billiambash.clotr@gmail.com](mailto:billiambash.clotr@gmail.com). I read every report, keep it private,
and reply within 7 days. If the problem is with me, say so in that email too, and I'll still answer it in writing.

What happens next follows the Covenant's enforcement steps: a private word, then a warning, then a temporary ban, and
for serious or repeated harm a permanent one. Whoever reported it hears what I did.
